Privacy

Your notes. Your browser. Your business.

NoteCraft has no backend and no database. When you write a note, it’s saved straight into your browser’s localStorage — a small storage space that belongs to the page, on your device. There is no server to breach, no account to compromise, and no analytics watching you type.

  • Works with Wi-Fi off

    Once the page has loaded, NoteCraft never touches the network again. Airplane mode is fine.

  • Nothing to hack or leak

    There’s no database of millions of notes somewhere. There’s just yours, on your machine.

  • Fast by physics

    Notes load from your own disk in milliseconds — not from a server across an ocean.

Honest caveat: local storage means real ownership. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes your notes permanently. That’s the trade-off for a tool that answers to no one.
